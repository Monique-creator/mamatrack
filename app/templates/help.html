Quick guide Ubufasha

Everything you need for a normal day, in six steps.

  1. Sign in with your username and password. After 5 wrong tries the account locks for 15 minutes, and you are signed out after 15 minutes without activity.
  2. Register a mother (nurses) with her name, delivery date and place of delivery. The delivery date cannot be in the future.
  3. Check the due date. MamaTrack adds 42 days to the delivery date. If you correct the delivery date, the due date changes too.
  4. Record the visit with the visit date and outcome. The case moves to Completed and leaves the overdue list.
  5. Add a risk indicator if you notice a danger sign. It is a note of concern, not a diagnosis, and it moves overdue cases to the top.
  6. Use the filters on the dashboard: Overdue, Due soon, Risk-tagged and Completed, or search by name or ID.

Colours

  • Overdue due date passed, no visit recorded
  • Due soon due within the next 7 days
  • On track follow-up is still ahead
  • Completed visit recorded by staff
  • Needs review two different visits were synced

For CHWs in the field

Open Field visits and tap Log visit. With no network the update shows Pending sync and is kept on your phone for at least 14 days. It is sent automatically when the network comes back.
