MamaTrack

No mother missed after the six-week mark.

MamaTrack helps health centre staff and community health workers see which mothers are due or overdue for their postnatal visit, and which ones carry a recorded risk.

Sign in Injira Request an account
  • Register the mother at delivery.
  • Day 42 follow-up date is set for you.
  • Overdue cases turn red on the dashboard.
  • Risk tags push urgent cases to the top.

Why it matters

Only about 57% of postpartum women in Rwanda receive any postnatal care. The first six weeks after birth are the riskiest, and today the follow-up list lives in spreadsheets.

Who uses it

Nurses and midwives manage the facility list, CHWs log visits from the field even with weak network, and supervisors watch overdue and risk numbers across facilities.
